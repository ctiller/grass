# Code of conduct

Grass welcomes rigorous disagreement. Architectural claims, specifications,
proof sketches, assembly, and review conclusions may all be challenged. People
must be treated with respect while that work is challenged.

Participants are expected to:

- argue from evidence and state uncertainty;
- criticize artifacts and decisions rather than people;
- give actionable review and allow reasonable time for response;
- respect privacy, attribution, and disclosure boundaries; and
- help maintain a technically serious, harassment-free environment.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, and retaliation for good-faith
reporting are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban a
participant when needed to protect the project and its community. Enforcement
decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid exposing a
reporter or other affected person.

Report conduct concerns through the private route described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not place private or safety-sensitive details in
a public issue or agent-bus event.
